For each question, use the chips to show your thinking. Tap + Chip to add disks. Tap ones to select ten, then press Trade 10 ones → 1 ten. Type your answer and choose Check answer.

Chips are a workspace: select 10 matching disks (they turn gold), then trade them for one disk in the next place. The trade buttons also work automatically when you have at least ten. Tap a selected chip again to unselect it. Answers are graded automatically; models are not graded.
